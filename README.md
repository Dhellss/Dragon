## Project 1 — Dragon

**Vibe:** heaviest, most cinematic

A 3D serpent-dragon circles a glowing orb in the middle of the screen (three.js), lighting the whole page. Content sits in terminal-style windows on the left and right. A CRT scanline overlay sits over everything.

- **Pages:** home · projects · contact
- **Interactions:** click the dragon and it reacts; on the contact page a firefly jar releases fireflies that follow the dragon; copy buttons for email and GitHub.
- **Detail:** each page has its own glow colour — orange (home), green (projects), violet (contact).
- **Tech:** three.js r128 from CDN, two `<canvas>` layers, JetBrains Mono.
- **Note:** this is the only project that needs internet for its 3D script.
